Import operators from Excel

Pick an .xlsx file. Every sheet becomes a site, and the columns CODE NO.NAMEFATHER NAME ACCOUNT NUMBERIFSC CODE are read automatically. Existing operators are matched by code and updated — nothing is lost.

Standard month length

The supervisor types WD only — OT and AB are worked out from it:
WD < standard → AB = standard − WD WD > standard → OT = WD − standard WD = standard → both blank
OT and AB are calculated, not editable — the supervisor cannot get them wrong.

Quick fill

Sets WD for every operator on this site who has nothing entered yet for the selected month — OT and AB follow from the formula. Operators already filled in are untouched.

Load my setup file

Head office sends you a small .json file on WhatsApp. Loading it puts your own sites and your operators into the app. Nothing you have already typed for this month is lost.

Backup & restore

A backup holds every site, operator and month of entries in one small .json file. Keep one before handing the phone to someone else.
